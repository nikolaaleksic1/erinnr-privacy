Privacy Policy for erinner

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how erinner ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and processing your data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable EU/EEA laws.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

Application: erinner
Contact Email: aleksicapps@gmail.com

2. About erinner

erinner is an AI-powered task management application designed to help users quickly capture and organize tasks. Our app uses voice-to-text input, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for photos, and AI models powered by Mistral AI to convert your spoken words or photos into structured tasks with titles, descriptions, and due dates. Users authenticate securely via email OTP.

3. Information We Collect & Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Email Address: Collected during login and registration via email OTP (one-time password) for secure account creation and authentication. (Legal Basis: Performance of a Contract - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Voice/Audio Data: Temporarily recorded when you use voice-to-text to generate tasks. Audio files are processed in real time and are not permanently stored. (Legal Basis: Consent - Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
  • Photos & Document Data: Captured via camera or photo library for OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract text and auto-generate tasks. Images are discarded after processing. (Legal Basis: Consent - Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
  • Task Content: Task titles, descriptions, due dates, and reminders created within the app. (Legal Basis: Performance of a Contract - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising ID: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens and Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) used for delivering task notifications and app performance diagnostics. (Legal Basis: Consent / Legitimate Interest - Art. 6(1)(a/f) GDPR)

4. Device Permissions

  • Microphone: Used for voice-to-text task creation. Audio is processed in real time and not stored permanently.
  • Camera & Photos: Used exclusively for OCR text extraction from photos to generate tasks, or scanning QR codes. We do not store or share your personal photo gallery.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver push notifications and task reminders configured by you via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Storage: Used to save your local task cache and app preferences on your device.
  • Biometrics: Optionally used for faster app unlock via fingerprint or Face ID. Biometric data remains secure on your device and is never transmitted.

5. AI Data Processing

Text generated from your voice or OCR photo scans is processed by Mistral AI to structure tasks automatically. Your data is processed strictly via API calls and is not used to train public AI models.

6. Third-Party Services

erinner relies on the following third-party service providers to deliver app functions:

  • Mistral AI — AI model processing to structure task titles, descriptions, and dates. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase / Google Play Services — Email authentication, FCM push notifications, app diagnostics, and Advertising ID management. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Manages in-app subscriptions and purchase validation. Privacy Policy

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and task data for as long as your account remains active. Temporary voice recordings and image files are deleted immediately after text transcription and task extraction complete.

You may request full deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at aleksicapps@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you reside in the EU/EEA, you hold the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your personal data ("Right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18): Request restrictions on how your data is processed.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Request your data in a portable electronic format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): Revoke permission for camera, microphone, or push notifications at any time in device settings.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local EU Data Protection Authority.
