Privacy Policy for Erinnr App

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Erinnr App ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using Erinnr App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. About Erinnr App

Erinnr App is a task management application that allows users to create tasks using voice-to-text input. Our AI (powered by Mistral AI) automatically structures your spoken input into well-formatted tasks with titles, descriptions, and due dates. Users can authenticate via email OTP and add tasks by scanning QR codes.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address: Collected during registration and login via OTP (one-time password) for authentication purposes only.
  • Voice/Audio data: Recorded temporarily to convert speech to text for task creation. Audio is not stored permanently.
  • Task data: Titles, descriptions, due dates, and other task content you create within the app.
  • Device information: Basic device data used for app functionality and crash reporting.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used exclusively for scanning QR codes to add or import tasks. We do not capture, store, or transmit photos or videos.
  • Microphone: Used for voice-to-text task creation and speech recognition. Audio is processed in real time and not stored.
  • Storage: Used to save your tasks and app preferences locally on your device.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver task reminders and alerts you set within the app.
  • Biometrics: Optionally used for faster login via fingerprint or Face ID. Biometric data never leaves your device.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you securely via email OTP
  • To process voice input and generate structured tasks using Mistral AI
  • To send task reminders and notifications you configure
  • To manage your subscription through RevenueCat
  • To improve app performance and fix issues via Firebase

5. Third-Party Services

Erinnr App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Mistral AI — Processes voice/text input to generate structured task content. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) — Used for push notifications and crash analytics. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Manages in-app subscriptions and purchase validation. Privacy Policy

6. Data Storage and Security

Your task data is stored locally on your device and, where applicable, on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Erinnr App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
