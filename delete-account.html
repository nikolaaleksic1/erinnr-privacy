Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly inside the Erinnr App:

  1. Open the app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

This will immediately and permanently delete:

  • Your account and profile
  • All your tasks and data
  • Your email address from our system

Alternatively, you can email us at: aleksicapps@gmail.com
